# Commercial License

OpenStageRF is dual-licensed. The default license is AGPLv3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

A commercial license is available for any of the following uses:

- Shipping a closed-source firmware build of OpenStageRF in a hardware product
- Embedding OpenStageRF in a proprietary software product without releasing the surrounding code under AGPLv3
- Any other use that is incompatible with the obligations of AGPLv3

Commercial licenses are negotiated per-project. Typical terms include a per-unit royalty, an annual fee, or a flat buyout, depending on the use case and scale.

## Why this exists

The commercial track funds:

- FCC and equivalent regulatory certification for OpenStageRF-branded hardware (single-digit-thousands-of-dollars per design even on the easiest pre-certified-module path)
- Ongoing development and maintenance of the open-source firmware
- Reference hardware designs and integration support

It is **not** intended to monetize end users, performers, hobbyists, or small builders working on their own rigs — those uses are fully covered by AGPLv3 at no cost. The commercial license is specifically for companies productizing the firmware in closed-source hardware.

## What about contributors?

Contributors to the project grant the maintainer the right to redistribute their contributions under both AGPLv3 and the commercial license (currently via DCO sign-off; a formal CLA will be added before external contributions are merged). Royalty income from commercial licenses is retained by the maintainer to fund the project. If contribution becomes substantial enough that revenue sharing makes sense, that is open to discussion.
